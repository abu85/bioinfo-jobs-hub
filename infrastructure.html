Infrastructure Directory

A curated, continent-organised list of bioinformatics infrastructure worldwide — international bodies first, then national infrastructures and research institutes grouped by continent. Still representative rather than exhaustive: infrastructure genuinely concentrates in Europe, North America and East Asia, which is why those sections are longest — not because other regions lack capable groups. Missing something obvious? This list is meant to grow.
