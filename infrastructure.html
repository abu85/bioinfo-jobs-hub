Infrastructure Directory

A representative, curated set of the world's major bioinformatics infrastructures — not an exhaustive database. Organised from international bodies down to national infrastructures and university-level core facilities. Missing something obvious? This list is meant to grow.

International & global bodies

EMBL-EBI

European Bioinformatics Institute — genomics, proteomics and chemistry databases, tools and training used worldwide.

Hinxton, UK

NCBI

US National Center for Biotechnology Information — GenBank, PubMed, BLAST and core sequence databases.

Bethesda, USA

DDBJ

DNA Data Bank of Japan — the Asia-Pacific node of the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration.

Mishima, Japan

ELIXIR

Pan-European infrastructure coordinating life-science data across ~20+ national nodes.

Europe-wide

ELIXIR TeSS

Searchable catalogue of bioinformatics training events and materials worldwide.

Europe-wide
